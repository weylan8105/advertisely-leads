Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Essential Workers Wealth Solutions (the "Program") is operated by Advertisely ("Advertisely," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information we collect when you fill out one of our forms on Facebook or Instagram, how we use it, and who we share it with. It applies only to the Program.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit a form we collect your name, phone number, email address, state, and your answers (for example your trade or healthcare role, age range, household income, when you would like to retire, and how much you could set aside each month).

Consent records. When you submit, we keep a record of your consent, including the consent language shown to you and the date and time.

Information from Meta. Meta tells us which ad, campaign, and form you responded to, along with an identifier for your submission. This page does not use cookies or tracking tools.

How we use information

  • To respond to your quote request and connect you with licensed insurance professionals who can discuss coverage options with you.
  • So you can be contacted by phone, text message, and email about your request, as described in the consent you gave.
  • To keep records of your consent and requests, and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, including telling Meta how far a request progresses (for example an application or a completed sale) so it can measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, spam, and abuse, and to keep our systems secure.

How we share information

  • Insurance professionals. With your consent, we share the information you submit with Enhanced Wealth and participating licensed insurance agencies, agents, brokers, carriers, or other insurance providers that may offer products or services matching your request. These companies may contact you and use your information under their own privacy policies. They may pay us for your request, so this may be considered a "sale" of personal information under some state laws.
  • Service providers. With companies that help us run the Program and deliver your request, such as website hosting, databases, email delivery, customer-relationship management, spreadsheets, and messaging tools. They may use the information only to provide those services.
  • Advertising partners. Meta receives the information you enter in its forms. We may also send Meta your email address and phone number in hashed (encoded) form, your submission identifier, and updates on your request, including the premium of a completed sale, so it can match and measure our ads. Meta's use of information is governed by its own policies.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of you, us, or others.
  • Business transfers. As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of our business.

Your choices

  • Calls and texts. You can stop calls or text messages at any time by telling the person who contacts you, replying STOP to a text message, or emailing us. Message and data rates may apply. Your consent is not required to purchase any product or service.
  • Email. Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or email us.
  • Ads. You can manage your ad preferences in your Meta account settings.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Virginia, and other states with privacy laws), you may have the right to: know what personal information we collect and how we use and share it; access a copy of it; correct it; delete it; and opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising. You also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Where the law provides one, you may appeal our decision on your request.

To make a request, including a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, email support@advertisely.io with the subject "Privacy request." We'll verify your request using information you've given us (such as your phone number and email address) before acting on it. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows it.

Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including keeping consent and request records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations, and then delete or de-identify it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. No website or transmission is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Program is intended for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version here with a new effective date.
