What You Get

Why Contractors & Skilled Trades Choose This

Most trade work leaves you to build your own safety net — no pension waiting at the end, and nothing that keeps paying if a serious illness takes you off the job. Here's what an indexed universal life policy is designed to do about that.

Money If a Serious Illness Strikes

A serious diagnosis pulls you off the job? Living benefit riders let you access part of your death benefit early after a qualifying condition — so money is coming in even when you can't work.

Cash Value You Can Use Later

Your money grows tax-deferred and stays yours to use — a kid's college, a work truck, your retirement. No waiting until 59½, no forced withdrawals down the line.

Protected From Market Crashes

When the market climbs, your cash value grows with it. When it crashes, that loss doesn't come out of your policy — so you're not digging back to even before you can grow again.

FAQ

Questions? Start Here.

Can I qualify if I have a health condition? +
Often, yes. Many blue-collar workers with common health conditions still qualify — carriers weigh a lot of factors, not just one. A licensed agent reviews your situation and matches you to carriers most likely to approve you. There's no cost or obligation to find out.
How much does an IUL policy cost? +
There's no set price — what you pay depends on your age, your health, how much coverage you want, and how the policy is structured. That's exactly what your free quote is for: a licensed agent designs an option around a budget that's comfortable for you.
Does this work if I'm self-employed, 1099, or W-2? +
Yes. It doesn't matter whether you run your own crew, drive 1099, or clock in W-2. An IUL is an individual policy you own — it isn't tied to an employer, so it stays with you no matter where or how you work.
What happens after I submit my information? +
A licensed independent agent reaches out — usually within 24 hours — to go over your options. No pressure and no obligation to buy. If it's a fit, they'll help you get covered; if not, you'll still walk away knowing your options.
