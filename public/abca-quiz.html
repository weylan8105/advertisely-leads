The strategy

What is an IUL?

An Indexed Universal Life (IUL) policy is permanent life insurance with a built-in savings engine. Part of what you put in protects your family if something happens to you. The rest grows tax-free, tied to the market's gains but shielded from its losses. It's how a lot of tradesmen build a retirement they can actually count on, without gambling their future on the stock market.

Three things it does at once

Tax-free retirement income

Your cash value grows tax-free, and it's built so you can pull income in retirement without handing a chunk to the IRS.

Protection for your family

A permanent death benefit that pays out to the people who depend on you, so one bad day never leaves them behind.

Living benefits

Get seriously hurt or sick and can't work? You can access money from the policy while you're still alive, right when you need it most.
