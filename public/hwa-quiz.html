Why Nurses & Healthcare Workers Choose This

You spend every shift protecting other people. Here's what an indexed universal life policy is designed to do for you and your family.

Protect your family if you pass away.

A life insurance death benefit can help your family cover the mortgage, everyday bills, and future plans if you pass away while the policy is in force.

Money if a serious illness takes you off your shifts.

Living benefit riders can let you access part of your death benefit early after a qualifying serious illness, so money is available when you can't work.

Conditions, limits, and costs vary. Accelerated benefits reduce the remaining death benefit and may reduce cash value. This is not a substitute for disability income insurance.

Grow and protect your money for the future.

Cash value can build over time, with interest linked in part to a market index and a floor that limits index-linked losses.

Growth is not guaranteed. Policy charges, loans, and withdrawals can still reduce cash value, increase lapse risk, and have tax consequences.

Straight answers before you apply.

What happens after I request a quote? +
A licensed agent follows up to discuss your needs, budget, and options. Submitting the form does not create coverage, guarantee approval, or commit you to buying a policy.
How much does an IUL policy cost? +
Cost depends on factors such as age, health, coverage amount, riders, and policy design. Ask for a personalized illustration showing premiums, charges, and guaranteed and non-guaranteed values. The quote request is free. The policy is not.
I already have life insurance through my hospital. Do I need this? +
An individual policy is separate from employer coverage and generally stays with you if you change jobs, go per diem, or take travel assignments. Review your existing benefits and discuss any gaps with an agent. Keep existing coverage in place while comparing options.
Does this pay whenever I cannot work? +
No. Certain riders may allow early access to part of the death benefit after a qualifying illness. Being unable to work does not automatically qualify you. Ask about disability income insurance if replacing income is your goal.
Can my cash value go down? +
Yes. An interest-crediting floor does not eliminate policy charges. Charges, loans, and withdrawals can reduce cash value. Caps and participation rates also affect credited interest. Review the actual policy terms and illustration.
What if I have a health condition? +
An agent can explain the underwriting process and discuss available options. Only the insurer can determine eligibility and pricing. Approval is not guaranteed.
